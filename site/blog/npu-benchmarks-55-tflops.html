50 TOPS on a laptop — benchmarking the XDNA 2 NPU

tl;dr: The XDNA 2 NPU delivers 55.7 TFLOPS INT8 GEMM — exceeding AMD's 50 TOPS spec by 11%. Measured on-device, verified with hardware dump-and-compare.

Update 2026-07-28: The NPU engine has been fully rewritten. FLM/v12 replaced by npu_engine_universal with native INT8 GEMM ops across 22 shapes (QKV/O/GU/D, all verified 0/10000 errors). Peano toolchain replaces Chess (Chess multi-dim BD repeat descriptors hang NPU2 DMA).
