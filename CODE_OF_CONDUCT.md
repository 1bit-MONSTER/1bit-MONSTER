# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the code, not the person.

## Expected

- Direct, technical, specific feedback.
- Good faith: assume the other person is trying to make the engine better.
- Measured claims. "This is slow" is an opinion; a benchmark is an argument.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Deliberate derailing, bad-faith argument, or repeated disregard for maintainer decisions.
- Publishing others' private information.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, delete, or reject comments and contributions, and may block accounts, for behaviour they judge to violate this document. Report problems by opening a private security advisory or contacting a maintainer directly.
